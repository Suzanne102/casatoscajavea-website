How to Get from Alicante Airport to Jávea

Alicante-Elche Miguel Hernández Airport (ALC) is the closest major airport to Jávea. You can continue by ALSA bus, taxi, private transfer or rental car. This guide explains the practical choices and how to reach Casa Tosca in the Old Town.

Quick comparison

  • ALSA bus: usually the most economical choice; check your date because routes and journey times vary.
  • Taxi or private transfer: the simplest door-to-door choice, especially with luggage or a late arrival.
  • Rental car: useful if you plan to explore the Costa Blanca, but Casa Tosca is in a pedestrian Old Town street.

ALSA bus from Alicante Airport to Jávea

Aena lists an ALSA connection between Alicante-Elche Airport and Jávea. Stops on the route can include Alicante, Alicante railway station, Benissa, Teulada and Benitachell. The exact itinerary, departure time and fare depend on your travel date.

  1. Search from Alicante Airport to Jávea on the official ALSA website.
  2. Check whether your selected journey is direct or includes a connection.
  3. At the airport, go to Level 2 (Departures). The outdoor intercity bus bays are in front of the terminal.
  4. From Jávea bus station, Casa Tosca is approximately a ten-minute walk in the direction of the historic centre.

Taxi or private transfer

A taxi or pre-booked transfer is the most convenient option if you arrive late, carry substantial luggage or travel as a group. Travel time and price depend on traffic, time of day and the provider, so confirm the total price before booking. During summer and holiday periods, reserve in advance.

Rental car

A rental car gives you flexibility for beaches, coves and nearby towns. Follow your navigation app toward Jávea/Xàbia, but use a public car park when you reach the Old Town. Carrer En Forn is a narrow pedestrian street and has no guest parking.

  • Nearby parking: Parking Constitución
  • Alternative: Parking del Clot
  • Luggage drop-off: Calle Virgen del Pilar, then continue to a public car park.

What about the train or TRAM?

Alicante Airport has no mainline railway station. The C6 airport bus connects with Alicante city and Luceros TRAM station, but this is normally a more complicated route to Jávea than an ALSA coach or road transfer. It can still be useful if you are stopping in Alicante or travelling elsewhere along the coast.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a bus from Alicante Airport to Jávea?

Yes. ALSA sells airport–Jávea journeys. Services can vary by date, so confirm the complete itinerary in ALSA’s live search.

Where is the airport bus stop?

The intercity bus bays are outside Level 2, the Departures floor, in front of the terminal.

Does Jávea have a railway station?

No. For most visitors arriving at Alicante Airport, the bus, a road transfer or a rental car is more practical.
